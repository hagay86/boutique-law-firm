Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

We offer a free initial consultation for most personal injury and criminal defense cases. For other areas of law, such as corporate or family law, we offer a complimentary 15-minute phone screening to determine if we can assist you, followed by a paid detailed consultation.
Every situation is unique. The best way to determine if you have a case is to speak with one of our experienced attorneys. During the consultation, we will review the facts of your case, applicable laws, and advise you on the best course of action.
Our fee structure depends on the type of case. For personal injury, we work on a contingency fee basis, meaning you pay nothing unless we win. For criminal defense and civil matters, we typically charge an hourly rate or a flat fee depending on the complexity of the case.
Timelines vary significantly based on the nature of the case, court schedules, and the willingness of the opposing party to negotiate. A simple car accident claim might settle in a few months, while complex litigation can take years. We will keep you informed every step of the way.
